The Programme

Three rooms,
one violin

Most of my year is spent in three kinds of room: a ceremony space where twelve minutes of music has to land exactly on cue, a banqueting hall between speeches, and a hotel lounge at seven on a Thursday. Each one asks for something different from the instrument. Here is how I work in all three.

I

Weddings & Ceremonies

The ceremony is the part nobody gets to rehearse twice. I watch the door rather than the clock, so the processional stretches or shortens to match the walk. Everything is played live, including the song you send me three weeks before that nobody has ever arranged for solo violin.

  • Pre-ceremony seating music25 to 30 minutes
  • Processional, signing and recessionalYour choices, timed live
  • Drinks reception setUp to 60 minutes
  • One custom arrangementIncluded in the fee
  • Battery amplification for gardens and marqueesCarried as standard

II

Corporate & Gala Evenings

Awards dinners, charity galas, product launches and partner receptions. These nights run to a schedule written by someone else, so I take my cue from your event manager and the AV crew. I can play fully acoustic for a drinks hour, or with backing tracks and in-ear timing when the room is large and the brief is bigger.

  • Arrival and welcome music45 minutes
  • Dinner or reception setsThree sets of 40 minutes
  • Backing tracks or fully acousticYour call
  • Sound check before doorsCoordinated with your AV team
  • Public liability cover and PAT certificatesSent with the contract
Request a corporate quote
III

Hotel Lobby & Lounge Residencies

A lounge is a room where people are talking, and the music sits underneath that. I play at conversation level, rotate the programme so regulars hear something new, and set up in a corner with a footprint of about one square metre. Residencies run weekly, monthly or as a seasonal booking through December.

  • Evening residencyTwo or three hours, in sets
  • Programme rotationRefreshed every month
  • Own low-level amplificationNo house system needed
  • DressBlack tie or to your standard
  • Festive season programmesBooked from September

and the hour

Send the date, the venue and roughly how long you need music for. I reply with availability and a written fee within one working day, travel included. Saturdays between May and September go early, so it is worth asking well ahead.
