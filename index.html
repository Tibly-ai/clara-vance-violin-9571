Written for your run of show

I have played roughly four hundred events since 2011, most of them in London hotels, livery halls and barn venues across Kent, Sussex and the Cotswolds. The work is less about a set list and more about timing: how long the aisle actually is, when the registrar signs, how loud the room gets once the bar opens.

So every booking starts with a short call. We go through the schedule, pick the processional, decide what plays while people find their seats, and agree what happens if the ceremony runs twenty minutes late. I arrive an hour early, tune in the room, and coordinate with your planner or toastmaster so nobody has to look for me.

I perform acoustically for gatherings up to about eighty guests and bring a discreet pickup and battery amplifier for larger rooms, outdoor ceremonies and gala dinners. Backing tracks are available where a fuller sound suits the piece, and I regularly play alongside a pianist, harpist or cellist when you want a duo.

Where the violin fits

All performance types

01

Wedding ceremonies

Prelude music as guests arrive, the processional you chose, something quiet under the register signing, then a recessional that gets people moving. I hold the last note until you reach the end of the aisle, however long that takes.

Typical set: 45 to 60 minutes, acoustic or amplified

02

Galas & awards dinners

Music that sits under conversation during the reception, lifts for the entrance, and drops away cleanly when the host takes the microphone. I work to the production schedule and take cues from the stage manager.

Typical set: three 30-minute sets across the evening

03

Hotel lounges & residencies

Standing weekly or monthly slots in lobby bars and afternoon-tea rooms. Repertoire rotates so regulars hear something new, and I keep the volume where guests can still talk across a table.

Ongoing residencies available from two hours

04

Corporate & private events

Product launches, client receptions, milestone birthdays and memorials. I can learn a specific piece with three weeks' notice, which covers most requests for a company anthem or a song that meant something to someone.

Public liability cover and PAT certificates on file
