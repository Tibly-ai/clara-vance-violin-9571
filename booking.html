Step one

Check a date

Pick a slot for a short call, or reserve a provisional hold on a performance date. Calls run about twenty minutes: venue, timings, the pieces you have in mind and whether the room needs amplification.

  • Consultation calls, weekdays 10am to 6pm
  • Provisional holds kept for seven days
  • Venue visits arranged for larger galas

Online booking is loading. If the calendar does not appear, call or email and I will find you a slot.

Step two

Send the event details

The more you can tell me now, the faster I can confirm. Ceremony start time, how long you want music, the size of the room and whether there is power near where I will stand.

I reply to every enquiry within two working days, usually the same evening.

Your details are used only to answer this enquiry.
